Sleep in whole cycles. Wake up rested.

Sleep runs in roughly 90-minute cycles. Pick a wake time or start now, and Moonrise lines up bedtimes and wake times that land on a cycle boundary — with a 15-minute window to fall asleep built in.

Suggested wake times

How this works: one sleep cycle ≈ 90 minutes — light sleep, deep sleep, then REM. Waking at the end of a cycle, when sleep is lightest, feels easiest. Moonrise adds your fall-asleep window on top, then counts in whole cycles.

Sleep cycles vary from about 80–100 minutes and how quickly you drift off varies from night to night — treat these as gentle estimates, not medical advice. If you regularly sleep badly, talk to a doctor.
